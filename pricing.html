Straight Answers

Every question you'd ask
before you commit.

Do I need to be on GLP-1 to join?

No. GLP-1 medication is an optional add-on. Every tier of the Vitality Program stands on its own, physician care, IBT, nutrition, fitness, coaching, InBody scans, with or without medication. Medication is prescribed only when your physician determines it's the right clinical choice, and never automatically.

What insurance do you accept?

Vitality is currently in-network with Medicare, Medicaid, and Cigna, and actively credentialing with Humana, UnitedHealthcare, CareSource, and most other major insurances. Behavioral therapy, physician visits, and nutrition therapy are often insurance-covered. Even if we're still credentialing with your carrier, book a free benefits verification and we'll tell you exactly what you'd pay before you commit.

What if I've tried everything and gained it all back?

You're in the right place, that's the population we built this for. Repeated regain signals the structure was missing, not that you're missing something. The four-phase, twelve-month sequence with a real facility, one dedicated coach, and physician oversight throughout is specifically designed to be the structure your previous attempts didn't have.

Will I have to take medication forever?

Not with our approach. Phase 3 typically begins physician-guided tapering conversations, and Phase 4 focuses on maintenance without medication where clinically appropriate. Building the behavioral and fitness foundation first is exactly what lets the results outlast the medication.

What if the medication doesn't agree with me?

Your physician manages side effects proactively, dose adjustments, timing changes, or switching medications. If GLP-1 isn't right for you, the medication charge stops and your monthly returns to the program-tier price. You're never charged for medication you don't receive.

Can I pause the program?

Yes. Medication track can be paused anytime without penalty. The program itself can be paused if life requires it (medical, family, work). Your coach builds a re-entry plan when you're ready.

What if I miss a session or two?

Life happens, we don't punish you for it. Sessions reschedule, phases flex, and your coach adapts your plan to where you are. What matters isn't perfect attendance; it's consistent re-engagement. That's a core piece of the IBT curriculum.
