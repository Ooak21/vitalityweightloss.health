Do I have to choose a medication now?

No. You can request a call without choosing a program. Our team can explain the options; a licensed clinician will decide whether a prescription is appropriate after evaluating you.

Is this a commitment to start treatment?

No. This form asks our team to contact you. It does not enroll you, authorize a payment, or replace a clinical consultation.

Can I get care from home?

Vitality offers Nevada telehealth as well as in-person care at our Las Vegas clinic. The team can explain what is available for your needs.

What happens after the first month?

For Vitality Direct, semaglutide is $199 per month for months 2–6 after the $149 first month. Tirzepatide is $399 per month for months 2–6 after the $249 first month. Ask our team about ongoing pricing beyond month 6 and the services included in each program.

What if I want nutrition or fitness support?

Ask about the comprehensive Vitality program. It offers additional nutrition, behavior, fitness, and body-composition support with separate program pricing.

Can I read more before requesting a call?

Absolutely. Visit our main website to explore the team and services, or read the full Vitality Direct program details.
