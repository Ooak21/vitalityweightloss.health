Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before you start.

Do I really not need a telehealth appointment to start?

Correct. The Vitality Direct model uses an async, physician-reviewed questionnaire in place of a synchronous consult. If your submission is straightforward and you clearly qualify, Dr. DeBry approves and prescribes within 24 hours. If your case needs a live conversation, we will schedule a telehealth call at no extra cost before charging you anything.

What if I do not qualify?

You pay nothing. Your card is captured at intake but never charged unless Dr. DeBry clinically approves you. If you do not qualify, we tell you why and, when appropriate, recommend our flagship Vitality Program as an alternative path.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

Yes. All PHI is encrypted in transit and at rest. Our intake platform is HIPAA compliant. Dr. DeBry and our clinical team are the only people who review your submission. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing.

Where does the medication come from?

All compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide injections are prescribed by Dr. DeBry and dispensed by a licensed local 503A compounding pharmacy. You can ship to your door or pick up in person.

What is the 6-month protocol?

The clinical evidence for GLP-1 outcomes is strongest at the 6-month mark, which is why we structure the program that way. You are not locked in. Cancel any month with no penalty. If you cancel early, no further charges run.

What does the free app actually do?

Injection tracking with dose, site rotation, and timing. Weight and progress tracking. Provider messaging. Meal plan and workout plan built for GLP-1 users. Titration reminders. Telehealth check-in scheduling. All included in every track at zero extra cost.

What is included in the price?

Your monthly price includes the compounded injection medication, physician review and prescription, ongoing titration decisions, provider check-ins on request, and full access to the Vitality Thrive app with all features. Shipping is included. Pharmacy dispensing fees are included.

Can I switch from Sema to Tirz later?

Yes, if it is clinically appropriate. Dr. DeBry will walk you through it during a check-in. Pricing adjusts to the new track from the switch month forward.
