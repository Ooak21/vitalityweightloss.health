Semaglutide Chocolate Peanut Butter Mug Cake

Wondering how to satisfy a sweet tooth on an Ozempic diet plan without the sugar crash? Since we prescribe the active ingredient Semaglutide, we know heavy desserts can cause severe stomach upset. This protein mug cake uses whey isolate and powdered peanut butter to give you a rich, chocolatey treat that actively feeds your muscles!

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop chocolate whey protein powder
  • 1 tbsp PB2 (powdered peanut butter)
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder & 1/4 cup almond milk
  • 1 tsp sugar-free chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. 1In a microwave-safe mug, mix the protein powder, PB2, and baking powder.
  2. 2Stir in the almond milk until a thick batter forms.
  3. 3Microwave for 45-60 seconds. Top with sugar-free chocolate chips while hot.
