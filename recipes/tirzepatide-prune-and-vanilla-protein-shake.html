Ingredients

  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • 3 pitted prunes (dried plums)
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • Dash of cinnamon & ice

Instructions

  1. 1Blend the prunes, almond milk, and ice on high until the prunes are completely pulverized.
  2. 2Add the protein powder and cinnamon, blending until creamy.
