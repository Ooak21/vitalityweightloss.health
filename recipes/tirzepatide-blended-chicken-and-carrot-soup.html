Tirzepatide Blended Chicken & Carrot Soup

If you are looking up Mounjaro dinner ideas for post-injection day, chewing heavy meats might sound unappealing. We prescribe Tirzepatide, and we highly recommend pureed soups. Blending chicken and carrots into a velvety soup gives you massive protein in a format that requires zero digestive effort.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz cooked chicken breast
  • 1 cup carrots, chopped
  • 2 cups chicken bone broth
  • Dash of thyme and salt

Instructions

  1. 1Simmer the carrots in the bone broth until they are completely mushy.
  2. 2Transfer the broth, carrots, and cooked chicken into a high-speed blender.
  3. 3Blend on high until completely smooth and velvety. Sip slowly.
