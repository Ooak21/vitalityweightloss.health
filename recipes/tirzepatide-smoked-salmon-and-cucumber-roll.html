Tirzepatide Smoked Salmon & Cucumber Roll

Wondering how to get more Omega-3s on Mounjaro without heating up the office microwave with fish? We prescribe Tirzepatide, and we highly recommend smoked salmon. Rolling it in thin cucumber strips gives you a sushi-like experience with zero carbs and zero cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cucumber, sliced thinly lengthwise (use a vegetable peeler)
  • 3 oz smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt or whipped cream cheese
  • Everything bagel seasoning

Instructions

  1. 1Lay the cucumber strips flat on a paper towel to absorb excess moisture.
  2. 2Spread a thin layer of Greek yogurt on the strips, top with smoked salmon, and roll up tightly.
  3. 3Sprinkle with seasoning.
