Tirzepatide Lean Beef Stuffed Cabbage

Looking for Mounjaro comfort food that won't derail your progress? We prescribe Tirzepatide, and we love stuffed cabbage. The cabbage provides phenomenal digestive support, while the lean beef ensures you are getting the protein needed to maintain your metabolism.

Ingredients

  • 4 large cabbage leaves, boiled until pliable
  • 4 oz 96% lean ground beef
  • 1/4 cup cooked rice
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce

Instructions

  1. 1Mix the raw ground beef and cooked rice. Divide and roll the mixture into the softened cabbage leaves.
  2. 2Place in a baking dish and cover with tomato sauce.
  3. 3Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 35 minutes until the meat is fully cooked.
