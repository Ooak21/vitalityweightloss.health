Tirzepatide Cottage Cheese Flatbread

If you are looking up Mounjaro lunch ideas that combat carb cravings, the viral cottage cheese flatbread is a must. We prescribe Tirzepatide, and we highly recommend this high-protein bread alternative. It provides massive protein and zero heavy starches to slow you down.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs or 1/2 cup egg whites
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder & Italian seasoning

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, and seasonings until smooth.
  2. 2Pour the mixture onto a parchment-lined baking sheet and spread into a thin rectangle.
  3. 3Bake for 30-35 minutes until golden and firm. Use as a wrap for deli turkey!
