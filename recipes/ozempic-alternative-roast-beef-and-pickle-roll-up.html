Ozempic-Alternative Roast Beef & Pickle Roll-Up

When researching an Ozempic diet, you'll find that small, frequent meals are best for severe appetite suppression. Because our clinic prescribes Semaglutide, we suggest skipping the bread entirely. These roast beef and pickle roll-ups offer instant protein, hydration, and electrolytes!

Ingredients

  • 4 slices ultra-lean deli roast beef
  • 1 tbsp whipped cream cheese
  • 2 pickle spears, halved

Instructions

  1. 1Lay the roast beef slices flat on a cutting board.
  2. 2Spread a very thin layer of cream cheese on each slice.
  3. 3Place a piece of pickle on one end and roll up tightly.
