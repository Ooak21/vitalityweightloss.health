Informed consent summary

You're consenting to once-weekly subcutaneous injections of compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, prescribed individually for you by Peter DeBry, MD (Nevada-licensed, NPI 1831137991) after asynchronous telehealth review of your intake.

What you can expect

Dr. DeBry reviews your intake within 24 hours. If approved, Dr. DeBry sends your prescription to a USA-licensed 503A compounding pharmacy, and we email you when it is ready to pick up. You self-administer weekly using the supplies provided with your prescription.

Common side effects

Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, decreased appetite, fatigue, injection-site reactions. Usually most pronounced during dose increases.

Serious risks

Pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, kidney injury, hypoglycemia (especially with insulin/sulfonylureas), severe allergic reactions, diabetic retinopathy complications, mood changes. Boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors (do not use if MTC/MEN-2 history). Tirzepatide may reduce oral contraceptive effectiveness for 4 weeks after starting and after each dose increase.

Compounded medication disclosure

Your medication is prepared specifically for you by a USA-licensed 503A patient-specific compounding pharmacy based in Las Vegas, NV.

This pharmacy operates under Section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. Each prescription is compounded individually under Dr. DeBry's individual order for you. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished drug products, although the active ingredient is FDA-approved in brand-name form.

You may decline the compounded version and request the FDA-approved brand product through your own physician. Vitality is happy to coordinate.
