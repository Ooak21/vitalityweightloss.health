01 / $50 PER PAID GROOMING MEMBERSHIP

The proposed payment is $50 per active, paid ChairCare grooming membership each month, even when the client does not use their haircut. One allocation per client per month. Medical enrollment alone does not trigger the payment.

02 / $100 MONTHLY FOR YOUR DISPLAY

A separate flat payment for the agreed banner and flyer placement, independent of signups. We provide the lobby materials. That is $1,200 annualized if the display agreement continues for twelve months.

03 / CARE OPTIONS FOR MORE CLIENTS

The proposed ChairCare package can pair the grooming benefit with weight care, Bloom hair care, men’s health, or primary care. Vitality handles the medical evaluation and explains package eligibility and terms directly to clients.

04 / YOUR QR CODE. YOUR PARTNER ACCOUNT.

A shop specific QR connects clients with Vitality. The planned private partner account will show eligible grooming memberships, haircut allocations, and payment history without displaying medical treatment details.
