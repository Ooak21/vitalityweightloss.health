Text Message (SMS) Program

Vitality Weight Loss patient care messages · Last updated: August 2026

DeBry Vitality Academies PC, doing business as Vitality Weight Loss, sends text messages from (702) 675-8982 to patients and prospective patients who ask to receive them. This page describes the entire program: how you opt in, what we send, how often, what it costs, and how to get help or stop the messages.

Message frequency varies — typically 2 to 6 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase or of receiving treatment.

How to opt in

There are four ways to join, and every one of them requires your own affirmative action:

1. Sign up on this page

Please add your email, your mobile number, and check the consent box.

You can stop at any time by replying STOP.

You’re signed up. Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

2. Check the SMS consent box on one of our web forms

Every form on this site that collects a phone number carries an unchecked SMS consent checkbox next to the phone field. You must check it yourself; nothing is pre-checked, and the form submits without it if you leave it blank. This is the box, exactly as it appears on our forms:

3. Text START to (702) 675-8982

Send the word START from the mobile number you want us to use. You will receive this confirmation and nothing further until you have a reason to hear from us:

“Vitality Weight Loss: You are subscribed to appointment and care reminders. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.”

4. Ask us in person or by phone

Give written or verbal permission at our clinic at 6140 Brent Thurman Way, Suite 100, Las Vegas, NV 89148, or by calling (702) 602-5002. Staff record the consent on your chart with the date and how it was given.

What we send

Messages are informational and transactional only: appointment reminders and confirmations, care follow-ups from your provider or care team, lab and body-composition scan notices, program and account notifications, and replies to questions you text us. We do not send marketing or promotional text campaigns.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your care, typically 2 to 6 messages per month. Some weeks you will receive none.

Cost

Vitality Weight Loss does not charge for text messages. Message and data rates may apply under your plan with your mobile carrier. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

HELP instructions

Reply HELP to any message for help. You can also call (702) 602-5002 or email info@vitalityweightloss.health. Our automatic reply to HELP is:

“Vitality Weight Loss: Support for appointment and care messages. Call (702) 602-5002 or email info@vitalityweightloss.health. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.”

STOP instructions

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. STOP, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, and QUIT all work. You will receive one final confirmation message and then no further texts from this program. To resubscribe, reply START. Our automatic reply to STOP is:

“Vitality Weight Loss: You have been unsubscribed and will no longer receive texts from DeBry Vitality Academies PC. Reply START to resubscribe.”

Opting out of text messages does not affect your care, your appointments, or any other way we contact you.

Privacy

How we handle your phone number and message content is described in our Privacy Policy, and the full program terms are in our SMS Terms. We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number, SMS opt-in consent, SMS content, or any SMS-related personal information with any third parties or affiliates for marketing, promotional, or any other purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Supported carriers

Compatible carriers include AT&T, Verizon Wireless, T-Mobile, and most US regional carriers. Carrier support can change without notice.
